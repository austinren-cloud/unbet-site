Questions

When can I get it?

We don't have a date yet, because we're still finishing the app and going through Apple's approval. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when it's ready.

Is it free?

The waitlist is free. The app will have a free version and a paid plan, Unbet+, which includes the panic button and blocker. Crisis help is always free.

Is my information private?

What you enter in the app stays on your iPhone. For the waitlist, we keep your email and use it only for launch news. Read how we handle it.

Is Unbet treatment?

No. Unbet is a self-help tool, not medical care or a crisis service. For confidential support, call or text 1-800-MY-RESET.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. We're starting with iPhone.
