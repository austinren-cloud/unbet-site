Unbet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version: everything you put into Unbet stays on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts and no tracking. We can't see your data, so we can't share or sell it.

What Unbet stores, and where

To work, Unbet saves the following only on your device:

  • Your answers from the setup questions (for example, which kinds of betting you did and roughly how much you lost each week)
  • Your start date, daily check-ins, logged slips and urges you beat
  • Your reasons for quitting, your note, your signed pledge and, if you choose one, a photo
  • If you choose one, the name and phone number of a person you trust, picked from your contacts
  • Your reminder settings

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Like any app's data, it may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple protects under its own privacy policy.

Photos and contacts

Unbet uses Apple's built-in pickers. You choose one photo or one contact, and only that item is shared with the app. Unbet never gets access to your whole photo library or contact list.

Payments

Subscriptions to Unbet+ are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, card number or Apple ID. The app only receives a yes or no from Apple about whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking across apps and websites
  • No selling or sharing of data, ever

Children

Unbet is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and in any case the app does not send information to us.

Changes

If a future version adds features that work differently (for example, optional accounts or connecting a bank), we will update this policy before that version is released and explain what changes, in plain language. You will be asked before any new kind of data leaves your device.
