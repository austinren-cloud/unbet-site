Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The short version: everything you put into the Unbet app stays on your iPhone. The app has no servers, no accounts and no tracking. We can't see your app data, so we can't share or sell it. The only information we receive is what you choose to send through the waitlist form on this website, explained below.

The website waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we save:

  • Your email address
  • Which link brought you to the form (for example, TikTok), and when you signed up

Because this could suggest you or someone close to you is dealing with gambling, we treat it as sensitive. We use it only to email you about Unbet's launch and to understand, as a group, where interest is coming from. We don't sell it or use it for advertising, including uploading it to ad platforms.

The list is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and we may use an email service to send launch emails. Those providers handle it only to run these services for us. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses. The site itself uses no analytics, ad trackers or cookies.

To be removed, or to see or correct what we hold, email unbetapp@gmail.com. We'll delete the list once it's no longer needed for the launch.

What Unbet stores, and where

To work, Unbet saves the following only on your device:

  • Your answers from the setup questions (for example, which kinds of betting you did and roughly how much you lost each week)
  • Your start date, daily check-ins, logged slips and urges you beat
  • Your reasons for quitting, your note, your signed pledge and, if you choose one, a photo
  • If you choose one, the name and phone number of a person you trust, picked from your contacts
  • Your reminder settings

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Like any app's data, it may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple protects under its own privacy policy.

Photos and contacts

Unbet uses Apple's built-in pickers. You choose one photo or one contact, and only that item is shared with the app. Unbet never gets access to your whole photo library or contact list.

Payments

Subscriptions to Unbet+ are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, card number or Apple ID. The app only receives a yes or no from Apple about whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups in the app
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking across apps and websites
  • No selling or sharing of data, ever

Children

Unbet is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, the waitlist is for people 18 or older, and the app does not send information to us. If you think someone under 18 has joined the waitlist, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If a future version adds features that work differently (for example, optional accounts or connecting a bank), we will update this policy before that version is released and explain what changes, in plain language. You will be asked before any new kind of data leaves your device.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email unbetapp@gmail.com.
